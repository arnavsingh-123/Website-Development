Mini Biography: Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer, former captain of the Indian national team, and one of the greatest batsmen in modern cricket.

Early Life

He was born in Delhi in 1988 and showed passion for cricket from a very young age.

Important Achievement

He led India to many victories and is known for his consistent batting records.

He is admired as the Run Machine because of his ability to chase and score under pressure.

One value that made him special was discipline and fitness.

Correction Corner

He only played cricket casually. He dedicated his life to professional cricket and leadership.

Fact 1: The Amazing Octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. The two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body.

How smart are octopuses?

Octopuses can open jars, solve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent invertebrates on Earth.

Fact 2: Lightning Strikes

Lightening strikes the Earth about 100 times every single second. This is roughly 8 million lightening strikes per day!
